Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how KTL Invoice ("we", "us", or "our") handles your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

KTL Invoice is designed to work offline. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to our servers. All data you enter (customer information, invoices, quotations, delivery orders) is stored locally on your device.

2. Google Drive Backup

Our app offers an optional backup feature that uses your Google Account to store backup files in a private folder within your own Google Drive (the "App Data Folder"). This means:

  • Backup files are stored in your own Google Drive
  • Only you can access these files
  • We cannot access your Google Drive or your data
  • You can delete the backup at any time from your Google Drive settings

3. Google Sign-In

To use the backup feature, you must sign in with your Google Account. We only request permission to access the App Data Folder in your Google Drive. We do not access any other files in your Drive.

4. How We Use Your Information

Since we do not collect any information, we do not use your information for any purpose. All data processing happens locally on your device.

5. Data Sharing

We do not share your data with any third parties. Your data stays on your device and, if you enable backup, in your own Google Drive.

6. Data Security

Your data is protected by your device's built-in security features and your Google Account's security. We recommend keeping your device and Google Account credentials secure.

7. Your Rights

You have full control over your data. You can:

  • Delete all data by uninstalling the app
  • Delete your Google Drive backups at any time
  • Revoke the app's access to your Google Account from your Google Account settings

8. Children's Privacy

Our app is not intended for use by children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
