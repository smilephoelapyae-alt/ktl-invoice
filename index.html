About the App

KTL Invoice is a mobile application designed for KTL Electrical Engineering to create, manage, and export professional invoices, quotations, and delivery orders. The app works offline and securely backs up your data to your own Google Drive account.

Key Features

  • Create Quotations, Delivery Orders, and Invoices
  • Manage customer database
  • Export to PDF and Excel
  • Track advance payments and balances
  • Convert Quote → DO → Invoice easily
  • Offline-first with Google Drive backup
  • Secure - your data stays in your Google Drive

Privacy & Data

Your data is stored locally on your device and optionally backed up to your own Google Drive account. We do not have access to your data.
